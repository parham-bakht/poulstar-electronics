🎯 How This Helps Your Scratch + Pi Pico Projects!

→

🔋 Voltage in Your Projects:

  • Pi Pico outputs 3.3V (perfect for most sensors!)
  • Some LEDs need exactly 3.3V
  • Too much voltage = broken components! 💥
  • Too little voltage = nothing works 😴

⚡ Current in Your Projects:

  • LEDs typically need 10-20mA
  • Motors need much more current
  • Pi Pico pins can give up to 16mA safely
  • Need more? Use a transistor or relay!

🧠 Key Things to Remember:

💡 Voltage is the "push" - like how hard you squeeze a water balloon
🌊 Current is the "flow" - like how much water comes out per second
🎯 In Scratch: You control voltage with digital pins (HIGH/LOW = 3.3V/0V)
⚠️ Safety: Always check component ratings before connecting!
